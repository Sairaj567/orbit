# 01 Product Specification

## Core Features
1. **Tasks**: Collaborative task management with statuses, priorities, and assignments.
2. **Habits**: Shared and individual habit tracking with streaks and gamification.
3. **Notes**: Collaborative text editing and organization.
4. **Study Blocks**: Shared focused study sessions with timers.
5. **Calendar**: Consolidated view of tasks, study blocks, and habits.
6. **Analytics & Activity**: Insights into productivity trends and workspace activity feeds.
7. **Achievements**: Gamified milestones for completing tasks and habits.
8. **Workspaces & Settings**: Multi-tenant environment configuration, roles, and preferences.

## Target Audience
Designed to scale from personal use (or pairs) up to large multi-tenant workspaces.

## Key Differentiators
- Real-time synchronization
- Modular "all-in-one" approach
- Gamification elements built-in
